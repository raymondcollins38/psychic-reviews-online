Good to Know

Frequently Asked

The platforms we rank are legitimate businesses with real advisors, vetting processes, and satisfaction guarantees. Whether any individual reader is genuinely psychic is a personal question — but the services themselves are real and refunds are honored.

Rates typically run from $0.66 to $4+ per minute depending on the advisor and platform. Most sites offer steep new-user discounts, so a first session can cost just a few dollars.

Reputable platforms offer 3–10 free introductory minutes to test compatibility. Genuinely "100% free" readings from unknown sources are usually low quality. Mysticsense is the only major site that requires no card to start.

California Psychics and Purple Garden consistently scored highest for relationship-focused sessions in our testing, thanks to advisors who specialize in love and compatibility.

We pay for real readings and score each platform across four weighted criteria: accuracy (35%), price and value (25%), advisor vetting (25%), and user experience (15%). Affiliate relationships never change the order.
