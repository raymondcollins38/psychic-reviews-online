Community Guidelines

Welcome to Psychic-Reviews-Online.com! We are a vibrant community dedicated to sharing honest, user-generated reviews of online psychic services. To ensure a positive, respectful, and helpful environment for everyone, we’ve established these Community Guidelines. By participating on our platform, you agree to abide by these principles, fostering a space where genuine experiences can be shared and informed decisions can be made.

Our Core Values: Respect, Honesty, and Helpfulness

At the heart of Psychic-Reviews-Online.com are three fundamental values that guide all interactions. We believe that by upholding these, we create the most valuable resource for individuals seeking authentic insights into psychic services.

  • Respect: Treat all members with courtesy and consideration, regardless of their opinions or beliefs. Disagreements are natural, but personal attacks, harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated. Remember, behind every username is a real person.
  • Honesty: Our platform thrives on genuine experiences. Please ensure your reviews are truthful, based on your direct interaction with the psychic service you are reviewing. Do not post fake reviews, propaganda for services you represent, or reviews designed to unfairly damage a competitor.
  • Helpfulness: The primary goal of our community is to help others. When writing reviews, provide as much detail as possible about your experience, including what you liked, what you didn’t, and why. Constructive feedback is always welcome and highly valued.

Crafting Effective and Fair Reviews

Your reviews are the backbone of our community. To ensure they are as helpful and fair as possible, please consider the following:

  • Be Specific: Instead of general statements, provide concrete examples. What specific advice did the psychic give? How did they deliver it? What was the outcome?
  • Focus on Your Experience: Your review should reflect your personal interaction with the psychic or service. Avoid making broad generalizations about entire platforms or groups of psychics based on one experience.
  • Maintain Objectivity (where possible): While reviews are inherently subjective, try to present your experience in a balanced way. Acknowledge both positive and negative aspects if applicable.
  • Avoid Personal Information: For your safety and privacy, do not include personal contact details (yours or others’), addresses, or highly sensitive personal information in your reviews.
  • No Commercial Solicitation: Do not use our platform to promote your own psychic services, affiliate links, or any other commercial ventures. This includes embedded links or overt self-promotion within reviews or comments.

Content Moderation and Reporting

To uphold these guidelines, our team actively moderates content posted on Psychic-Reviews-Online.com. We reserve the right to remove any content that violates these guidelines without notice. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Reviews or comments containing profanity, obscenities, or sexually explicit material.
  • Content that promotes illegal activities or incites violence.
  • Spam, repetitive posts, or off-topic discussions.
  • Impersonation of other users or psychics.
  • Content that infringes on intellectual property rights.

If you encounter content that you believe violates our Community Guidelines, please use the “Report” function available on each review or comment. Our moderation team will review your report promptly and take appropriate action. Repeated violations of these guidelines may result in temporary or permanent suspension from the platform.

What a good review looks like

These guidelines describe the kind of review we consider helpful. They are writing guidance, not a submission process. A strong review gives the next reader something they can use, and it stays inside what you personally experienced.

  • State what you used, when, and what you were hoping for, in plain words.
  • Describe facts first: price, length, wait time, and how billing worked.
  • Separate fact from feeling. “I was charged for ten minutes after the call ended” is a fact; “it felt cold” is an impression.
  • Mention what went well as well as what did not.
  • Say whether your expectations were realistic.

Acceptable and unacceptable wording

Fair criticism names a behaviour, not a person. “The reader kept steering me towards a longer session” describes conduct. Calling someone a fraud with no basis can be unfair and may be removed. Equally, praise should not read like an advert. Gushing, repetitive language with no details is less useful than a measured note about a clear price and a helpful response.

Language and tone

  • No harassment, slurs, or attacks on other contributors.
  • No threats or calls to harm anyone.
  • No mockery of anyone’s grief or beliefs.
  • No accusations of crimes unless you have documented evidence and keep the wording factual.

Privacy, health, and sensitive subjects

Reviews about readings can touch painful topics. Please avoid naming family members, giving medical details, or sharing identifying information about yourself or others. If you mention a health or legal question, describe it in general terms. Take particular care with anything about a minor.

Conflicts of interest

If you work for, are paid by, or are related to someone connected to a service, say so or do not review it. The same applies to rivals. Reviews written to boost or damage a business rather than to inform readers are the kind of content we do not want.

Why moderation exists and what it cannot do

Moderation aims to keep conversations useful and respectful. It cannot verify every claim, and a published review is the opinion of its author. Readers are encouraged to look for patterns across many accounts rather than any single one, and to read our review-writing tips and FAQ.

Frequently asked questions

Can I criticise a psychic or platform?

Yes, if the criticism is honest, based on your own experience, and avoids personal attacks.

Should I include screenshots or personal details?

Avoid anything that identifies you or others. Describe what happened in words instead.

What if I disagree with a moderation decision?

Moderation is judgement, not certainty. Consider rewording your experience in line with these guidelines.
